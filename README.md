# My Special

A mobile-first grocery basket comparison marketplace for Kagiso and Krugersdorp, South Africa.

## MVP status

- Separate Supabase project and database from MR-AWAY-FIX.
- Customer accounts can create grocery baskets with requested product, brand, size, quantity, area and target budget.
- Approved shops can submit itemized offers with delivery fees and substitutions.
- Customers can compare submitted offers and choose a shop.
- Initial MVP payment flow is direct payment to the shop; My Special does not collect or hold customer funds.
- New shop accounts require manual approval; no fake retailer offers are seeded.

## Important

The website is not considered launched until the frontend is published, Supabase Auth redirect settings are configured, and the customer/shop workflow is tested with real accounts. Review privacy, terms, refunds and POPIA obligations before public rollout.
